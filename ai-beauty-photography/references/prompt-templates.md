# Prompt template

Fill the blanks only; add no other sentences. The place is a real location and moment, not a styled studio or abstract backdrop, unless the brief asks for a studio.

```text
Create one [placement ratio, else 4:5] high-end beauty advertising photograph for [product, likely size, plainly].
Set it in [a real place at a specific moment, lit by its own real light], feeling like a luxury cosmetics campaign: [two or three mood words].
The model is [the cast person: age, hair, skin tone, eyes, one distinctive feature, not freckles or blemishes unless the brief asks for them], strikingly beautiful.
Compose it with [her position and how much of her is seen], keeping [open scene space (sky, wall, shadow, soft background) at the side, top or bottom per ratio] for logo and headline, without shrinking the beauty result.
Show [the makeup or product result] as the hero look; make [its colour, finish or effect] rich and unmistakable, the product held by her or in the foreground, in elegant unbranded packaging.
Real skin texture, crisp detail. No logos, brand names, monograms or readable text; no extra products, collage or variations.
```

For a product-only image, drop the model line and the words "held by her or", and fill the composition blank with the product's position instead of hers. For a later Shot in a Series, begin with "Image 1 is the identity reference for the model." and keep the rest.
